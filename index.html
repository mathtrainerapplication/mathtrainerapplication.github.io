🎮 10 math games in one app

Big buttons, bright pictures and cheerful sounds make learning feel like playing. Here are some of the fun ways to practice:

🦠

Number Bonds

Find two funny microbes that make the target number. Master one number to unlock the next—step by step.

➕

Add & Take Away to 10

Connect each example to the right answer. Quick rounds build confident mental math.

💯

Add & Take Away to 100

Three rounds from easy to tricky: one-digit, two-digit, carrying and borrowing a ten.

🎈

Bigger or Smaller

Race against the clock and pop only the balloons that match the number rule.

🔺

Number Pyramid

Each brick is the sum of the two below. Fill the gaps and build the pyramid.

🔗

Number Chain

Follow steps like +5 or −3 and fill in the missing numbers to close the chain.

🍎

Picture Puzzles

Every picture hides a number. Solve each line and crack the puzzle.

🧵

Laces

Find every example that equals the number in the middle and tie it with a lace.
