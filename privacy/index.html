Privacy Policy for Math Trainer

Last Updated: October 19, 2026

1. Introduction

Math Trainer ("the App") is an educational mobile application designed for children aged 6 to 12 to help them practice and improve their mathematical skills.

We take children's privacy seriously and aim to minimize the personal information required to use the App. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected or processed through the App and its integrated services.

The App does not display advertisements. It offers optional, one-time in-app purchases that unlock additional content. The App does not require a subscription.

2. Information We Collect or Process

The App does not require users to create an account or provide their name, email address, or phone number to use its core educational features. However, the third-party services integrated into the App may collect or process certain technical and gameplay information.

Unity Analytics

Unity Analytics helps us understand how the App is used and improve its functionality. Depending on the configuration and events enabled, it may process information such as:

  • IP address;
  • unique installation identifier and other technical identifiers;
  • app usage and gameplay events;
  • technical information needed for analytics and service operation.

Gameplay events may include information about completed activities, learning progress, or interactions with features when these events are configured and sent by the App.

Unity Cloud Save

Unity Cloud Save is used to store supported game data in the cloud so that progress may be retained and accessed across devices. The service may process an authentication player ID, IP address, and the game data saved through the service.

Google Play Games Services

Google Play Games provides supported gaming features, such as achievements and player progress features, where enabled. Depending on the features used and the player's account settings, Google may process a gamer identity, achievements, scores, saved game data, device information, identifiers, IP address, and diagnostic or analytics information.

Local Storage

The App also stores learning progress and settings locally on the user's device. This may include completed tasks, results, unlocked content, and other information necessary for the App to function. Local data is stored on the device rather than automatically being sent to our servers. Data may also be synchronized with cloud services when the relevant features are used.

3. How Information Is Used

Information processed through the App and its integrated services may be used to:

  • provide the App's educational features;
  • save and restore learning progress;
  • provide achievements and other Google Play Games features;
  • understand app usage and improve the learning experience;
  • identify and fix technical problems;
  • maintain the security and reliability of the App;
  • process and restore eligible in-app purchases.

We do not use the App to display advertisements or to deliver personalized advertising.

4. In-App Purchases

Math Trainer offers optional one-time purchases to unlock additional educational content or features.

Payments are processed by Google Play. We do not directly collect or store payment card numbers or billing credentials. Google Play may process purchase records, transaction identifiers, purchase status, and related information to complete transactions and support purchase restoration.

Parents and legal guardians should supervise purchases made by children and use the purchase authorization and parental control features available through Google Play and their devices.

5. Third-Party Services

The App integrates services provided by third parties. These providers process information according to their own privacy policies and the services enabled in the App.

  • Unity Analytics and Unity Cloud Save — analytics, technical identifiers, and cloud storage of supported game data. Unity Privacy Policy .
  • Google Play Games Services — achievements, player identity, and supported gaming or progress features. Google Privacy Policy .
  • Google Play Billing — processing of in-app purchases. Google Payments Terms and Policies .

We do not sell children's personal information. The App does not integrate advertising services for displaying ads.

6. Children's Privacy and Parental Consent

Math Trainer is designed for children aged 6 to 12. We aim to limit data collection to information needed to provide educational features, save progress, maintain the App, and support the services described in this Policy.

The App does not ask children to provide their names or contact details as part of its core educational activities. Nevertheless, technical identifiers, gameplay information, and other data may be processed by the integrated services described above.

Where applicable law requires verifiable parental consent before personal information is collected from a child, we will obtain that consent before the relevant collection or processing takes place. Parents and legal guardians should review the privacy information and available controls for the services used by the App.

Parents or legal guardians may contact us with questions or requests concerning their child's privacy using the contact details below.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Learning progress and settings may be stored locally on the device. Local data may be deleted by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App, subject to the device's operating system and backup settings.

Data saved through Unity Cloud Save may remain in cloud storage until it is deleted in accordance with the service's applicable retention and deletion procedures. Uninstalling the App does not necessarily delete cloud-stored data.

Google Play Games data and purchase records are managed in accordance with Google's applicable policies and retention practices. Deleting local App data does not necessarily delete data stored by Google or Unity.

Parents or legal guardians may contact us at math.trainer.application@gmail.com to request assistance with privacy-related questions or deletion requests. We will review requests in accordance with applicable law and the capabilities of the relevant services.

8. Data Security

We and our service providers use measures designed to protect information processed through the App. Data protection measures depend on the relevant service and its configuration. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Privacy Rights

Depending on the user's location and applicable law, users and, where appropriate, their parents or legal guardians may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data, or to object to or restrict certain processing.

Where required by applicable law, we will provide appropriate information, consent mechanisms, and ways to exercise these rights. Requests may be submitted using the contact details in Section 11.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, its integrated services, or applicable legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with a revised update date.
